## Sidebar navigation and narrow layouts

- A compact `今日` / `Today` button is available on desktop and mobile, following the LLR language setting. It returns only the sidebar to today without opening or creating a note.
- Underlined date headings in the timeline open that day’s existing daily note. Missing past notes are not created.
- Desktop date controls stay together on the left. On mobile, Today stays in the same position when switching between tasks and timeline.
- At very narrow desktop widths, previous/next arrows stay visible until space is nearly exhausted. After they hide, their reserved space and the timeline margins shrink gradually instead of making the controls jump. All remaining actions fit a 200px pane.
- Tighter hour-label, activity and trailing spacing gives activity names more room in narrow panes while retaining the existing text sizes and time scale.

The timeline continues to show completed records. The separate experiment for running and planned activities is not included.

## Updating

Update LLR through Obsidian Community Plugins or BRAT. Minimum Obsidian version remains 1.7.2. No data migration is required.
