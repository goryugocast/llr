## Plans in the timeline

- The timeline shows planned activities in today’s and existing future daily notes. Browsing does not create notes or insert routines.
- Completed records and today’s running activity take priority. Dashed plans fill available time, and elapsed plans disappear.
- For routines, a reminder time can be shown in a different timeline hour when a later section is configured. Other candidates use section times and estimates.
- Expanded task records retain their title, original note links, times and memos, even when the band heading repeats the same title.
- Improved handling of malformed time entries and multiple running tasks, including long overnight sleep records.

Update through Obsidian Community Plugins or BRAT. Minimum Obsidian version remains 1.7.2. No data migration is required.
